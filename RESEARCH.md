# Research notes — making each illness specific

**Date:** 28 October 2026. Builds on `illnesses.md` (the global top-ten shortlist). Several entries there are broad categories (e.g. "diarrhoeal diseases", "anaemia from all causes"). A 3D simulation has to show *one* mechanism, so each category was narrowed to the single most common, most visual condition inside it. The headline number shown in the app is always labelled with what it actually counts.

## From broad category to specific condition

| # | Category in `illnesses.md` | Specific condition shown | Why this one | What the 3D scene shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Upper respiratory infections (12.8 bn episodes, 2021) | **Common cold — rhinovirus infection of the nose** | Rhinoviruses are the single largest cause of colds: roughly 30–50% of episodes across studies; one PCR study found ~50% ([J Clin Microbiol 1998](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC104573/)). | Nasal lining with beating cilia and mucus; viruses latch on (via the ICAM‑1 receptor); vessels widen and leak (stuffy nose); mucus floods out; white cells arrive. Symptoms come mostly from the body's response, not cell destruction ([pathophysiology review](https://read.qxmd.com/read/10567986/an-update-on-the-pathophysiology-of-rhinovirus-upper-respiratory-tract-infections)). |
| 2 | Diarrhoeal diseases (4.67 bn episodes, 2021) | **Norovirus gastroenteritis ("stomach bug")** | Leading cause of acute gastroenteritis across all ages: ~685 million cases a year, ~1 in 5 cases ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/norovirus/data-research/index.html)). | Small-intestine villi soaking up water; norovirus infects the tips, villi blunt and broaden, water leaks back out ([biopsy findings review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11309505/)). |
| 3 | Headache disorders (2.9 bn, 2023) | **Migraine (with aura)** | The most visual headache disorder; ~1.16 bn prevalent cases in 2021 ([GBD 2021 migraine](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12620191/)). Aura affects about a third of people with migraine. | Spreading wave across the occipital cortex (aura), trigeminal nerve activation, CGRP release and inflamed, widened meningeal vessels, throbbing pain signals. |
| 4 | Untreated caries, permanent teeth (2.26 bn) | **Dental caries (a cavity)** | Already specific. | Molar cut in half (enamel / dentin / pulp, gum, bone). *S. mutans* plaque + sugar → acid below the critical pH ≈ 5.5 ([KEGG H01331](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/H01331)) → white spot → brown cavity → reaches the pulp (toothache). |
| 5 | Anaemia, all causes (2.13 bn, 26.4%) | **Iron-deficiency anaemia** | Dietary iron deficiency is the leading cause of anaemia in GBD 2021 ([Lancet Haematol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10530561/)); a commentary puts it at up to ~66% of the burden (modelled as a residual cause, so treat as approximate). | Cut-open vessel: red cells become fewer, smaller (microcytic) and paler (larger central pallor, hypochromic); less oxygen reaches body cells. WHO thresholds: Hb < 12 g/dL (women), < 13 g/dL (men). |
| 6 | Hypertension (1.4 bn adults 30–79) | **Primary (essential) hypertension** | 90–95% of cases ([JCI](https://jci.org/articles/view/69296)). | Three-layer artery with a travelling pulse; pressure arrows grow; media thickens, lumen narrows, wall stiffens (smaller pulse), endothelial micro-injury. Readout uses WHO's ≥ 140/90 threshold. |
| 7 | MASLD (1.3 bn, 16.1%) | **MASLD — simple steatosis, with a glimpse of MASH** | Already specific. Definition: steatosis (> 5% of hepatocytes) + ≥ 1 cardiometabolic risk factor ([MSD Manual](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/hepatic-and-biliary-disorders/approach-to-the-patient-with-liver-disease/metabolic-dysfunction%E2%80%93associated-liver-disease-masld)). | Whole liver turns from dark red-brown to yellow-tan and swells; zoomed lobule shows micro- then macrovesicular fat pushing nuclei aside; late stage adds immune cells and scar fibres. |
| 8 | Obesity (1.1 bn) | **Obesity — adipocyte hypertrophy & inflammation** | Obesity is a whole-body disease; the clearest cellular story is fat-cell enlargement → death → macrophage "crown-like structures" ([review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8179930/)). | Fat cells swell several-fold, crowd, some die; macrophages ring them; inflammation signals enter capillaries. Copy avoids blame language and mentions genes, environment, sleep and medicines. |
| 9 | Severe periodontitis (1.09 bn) | **Severe periodontitis (stage III/IV)** | Already specific. Stage III ≈ pockets ≥ 6 mm, bone loss to the mid-root ([EFP staging](https://www.efp.org/publications/projects/new-classification/guidance/report-02b.pdf)). | Plaque → tartar below the gum line → gingivitis → pocket opens, bone crest and ligament are lost, tooth loosens. |
| 10 | Diabetes, all types (828 m adults) | **Type 2 diabetes — insulin resistance** | Roughly 90% of diabetes worldwide is type 2 ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/diabetes)). | Insulin "keys" dock on receptor "locks"; GLUT4 "doors" open and glucose enters. With resistance, more keys arrive but few doors open; glucose piles up. Readout uses the fasting ≥ 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L) criterion. |
| + | Asthma (363 m, 2023) — discussed in `illnesses.md` | **Allergic asthma attack** | Very common, especially in children ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/asthma)). | Airway muscle squeezes, lining swells and folds, mucus plugs form, airflow drops; peak-flow zones (green ≥ 80%, yellow 50–80%, red < 50%). |
| + | Your example | **Eosinophilic esophagitis (EoE)** | Rarer (e.g. 113 per 100,000 in Sweden, 2022 — [population study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11734667/)) and rising; included because it was the example in the brief. Diagnosis: ≥ 15 eosinophils per high-power field. | Glass body with the digestive tract; cut-open esophagus where eosinophils accumulate, rings/furrows/white exudates form, the tube narrows and the food bolus gets stuck. |

## Facts double-checked for the on-screen copy

- Adults catch about 2–3 colds a year; most colds clear in 7–10 days.
- Norovirus illness usually lasts 1–3 days; soap and water work better than alcohol gel.
- Migraine attacks last 4–72 hours; women are affected about 2–3× more often.
- After sugar, plaque pH drops within minutes and stays acidic for about 20 minutes or more (Stephan curve).
- WHO: 44% of adults with hypertension don't know they have it.
- MASLD was previously called NAFLD; 5–10% weight loss reduces liver fat.
- WHO obesity counts: 940 m adults and 170 m aged 5–19 (2024).

## Honest simplifications in the visuals

- **Scale is exaggerated.** Viruses (~30 nm) are drawn about a tenth the size of a cell instead of ~1/300; red cells are drawn large relative to vessels.
- **Numbers on the readouts are illustrative ranges**, anchored to real thresholds (140/90 mmHg, 126 mg/dL, 12–13 g/dL Hb, pH 5.5, 6 mm pockets, 15 eos/HPF, BMI 30, 5% hepatocytes) — not a model of any individual.
- **Timelines are compressed**: the slider spans days (cold, norovirus), hours (migraine) or years (hypertension, decay, gum disease, MASLD, obesity, diabetes).
- Anatomy is stylised: one molar shape, a schematic liver lobule, a simplified brain surface.

The app shows a disclaimer: simplified illustrations for learning, not medical advice.
